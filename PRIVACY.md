# Privacy policy: Factuur Grabber

Factuur Grabber runs entirely in your browser.

- **No servers.** The extension sends nothing to its developer or any third party. Its only network traffic is to `*.kpn.com`, in your own logged-in browser session, to list and download your invoices.
- **No credentials.** It never reads, stores or transmits your KPN password or SMS codes. You log in to MijnKPN yourself.
- **What it stores, locally only** (`chrome.storage.local`): which invoices it already saved (date, product label, amount, file name), the expected date of each product's next invoice, and the KPN ID shown in the popup. Removing the extension deletes all of it.
- **Downloads** go to your own Downloads folder (`Facturen/KPN/`).
- **Support outlines** (optional, only when you click "Save page outline for support") are saved as a file on your computer, with e-mail addresses, amounts and long numbers masked. You decide whether to send it to anyone.
